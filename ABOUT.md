# About the online tone generator

[Toolster tone generator](https://toolster.net/tone_generator) plays one steady sound at exactly the frequency you set. Musicians take it as a reference pitch, audio people use it to check speakers, headphones and rooms, and teachers use it to show what pitch is.

## Frequency range

The slider goes from 1 Hz to 20 kHz. Near the bottom it moves in small steps and higher up in bigger ones, so the low range is as easy to set as the high one. For exact values type the number: it accepts up to three decimals (0.001 Hz). The ×½ and ×2 buttons jump one octave down or up.

## Waveforms

- **Sine** is the pure tone with no overtones.
- **Square**, **triangle** and **sawtooth** carry overtones and sound brighter and louder at the same volume.

## Stereo

The balance button plays the tone in the left ear, the right ear or both. Handy when you need to find out which side of the headphones is broken or whether the speakers are wired the right way round.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Space**: start or stop
- **Left / Right arrows**: move the slider
- **Shift + arrow**: change the frequency by 1 Hz

## Why some tones are silent

People hear roughly 20 Hz to 20 kHz, and the top limit drops with age. Phone and laptop speakers also can't play deep bass, so a 30 Hz tone may be silent on them and loud on a subwoofer.

## Safety

Start quietly, especially in headphones and with square or sawtooth waves. Long high tones at full volume can tire your ears and damage small speakers.
